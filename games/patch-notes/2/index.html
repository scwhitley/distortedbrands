New game · Root Access: Granted

Meet Root Access: Granted.

Root Access: Granted is the third Distorted Games project, and it just moved from planning into production. You play a teenager who's good with computers, on a desktop that looks and works like a real one. The story happens in Chatter, the in-game messenger, where your friends start asking for favors that cross lines.

Night 1 starts with your best friend Jay. His algebra grade is a 54, report cards go out Friday, and the school grade site has an admin page with no login. What you type back changes your Ethics, your Heat and Jay's Trust.

If you go through with it, you actually type the hack. The script sits on screen in grey, and you type over it letter by letter. Every typo glows red and pushes the Trace bar closer to school IT catching you.

Night 1 is already playable in the prototype: a desktop with windows you can drag, Chatter, type-to-hack with a live Trace timer, the three meters and saving. Next up are a character creator, Mail and more Act 1 nights.
