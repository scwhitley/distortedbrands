Cozy Hacker is in testing.

Playtest signups open this month. Plus a first look at Servers Hate You.

Welcome to the first issue. Once a month I'll tell you what changed in our games, show you what it looks like, and let you know when you can play it. Two games this time, and one of them is almost ready for you.

Build log · Cozy Hacker

Development is done. Now we break it on purpose.

Cozy Hacker is fully built and in testing. You live in a run-down apartment with your cat, and each night you open the gig board and pick up jobs from people who need a tech person.

Your neighbor Mrs. Patel is usually first in line. Her movers unplugged everything, so you get the patch sheet, six cables, a clock, and a mistake counter. Every job costs energy, so you have to pick which gigs are worth the night.

Build log · Servers Hate You

Planning is done. The servers are being built to hate you.

Servers Hate You is now a 2D troll platformer, and it's in development. You play an IT worker running through a data center that does not want you there. Those hazard stripes are not decoration.
